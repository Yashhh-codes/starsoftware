## Brand & Style

This design system embodies structural clarity, technical precision, and modern enterprise reliability. Built for an AI-native document processing infrastructure, the interface balances ultra-refined developer tools with high-level financial-grade governance. It channels the benchmark editorial elegance of modern fintech: purposeful whitespace, rigorous baseline alignment, razor-sharp outlines, and high visual legibility.

### Tone & Character
- **Confident & Architectural:** Grounded in high-contrast optical slate typography, neutral structural backdrops, and deliberate information pacing.
- **Engineered & Precise:** Micro-borders, delicate dividing lines, and micro-radii communicate data integrity and enterprise rigor.
- **Discreetly Dynamic:** Vibrant spectrum accents (violet-to-cyan) are restrained to focal interaction cues, status conduits, and brand moments, avoiding visual noise.

### Style Directives
- **Visual Foundation:** Crisp multi-tiered light backdrops, hairline boundary strokes (`#E3ECF7`), and ultra-diffused atmospheric ambient drop shadows.
- **Editorial Contrast:** High-contrast deep navy text hierarchies against pure white and porcelain wash backgrounds, pairing technical lucidity with executive-level presentation.

## Colors

The system uses an intentional, high-contrast light palette engineered for intensive document processing workflows and clarity across data-heavy tables.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Indigo (`#635BFF` / `#533AFD`):** Reserved for primary calls-to-action, active segment markers, key metric callouts, and interactive states.
- **Secondary Slate (`#50617A`):** Used for supporting metadata, field headers, secondary labels, table footers, and neutral inactive indicators.
- **Tertiary Cyan (`#00D4FF`):** Paired with the primary indigo in linear gradient accents, system status flashes, and active machine-learning ingestion queues.
- **Dominant Neutral Slate / Navy (`#061B31`):** Applied to core headings, primary metrics, high-emphasis text strings, and selected row states.

### Surfaces & Boundaries
- **Base Canvas:** `#F4F7FB` (cool porcelain background).
- **Surface Elevation 1 (Cards, Workspaces):** `#FFFFFF` (pure optical white).
- **Surface Elevation 2 (Sidebars, Inset Panels):** `#FBFCFD` (subtle off-white sheet).
- **Structural Lines & Hairlines:** `#E3ECF7` (crisp, low-saturation slate border) and `#E2E8F0` (standard utility dividing lines).

### Accent Ribbons & Gradients
- **Linear Spectrum Ribbon:** `linear-gradient(90deg, #635BFF 0%, #00D4FF 100%)`. Applied strictly to top-edge card highlights, processing timeline states, and brand verification elements with a 2px maximum height.

## Typography

Typography prioritizes geometric discipline and structural balance, utilizing **Hanken Grotesk** across displays, paragraphs, and micro-labels to emulate modern neo-grotesque engineering. **JetBrains Mono** handles tabular indices, confidence scores, payload identifiers, and document metadata.

### Typesetting Directives
- **Tight Tracking on Large Scales:** Negative letter-spacing tightens display headers (`-0.03em` to `-0.015em`), achieving high cohesion and impact.
- **Tabular Numerics:** Enable `font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1` across all dashboard metrics, confidence intervals, financial calculations, and data grids.
- **Purity of Hierarchy:** Never combine more than two font weights in a single component. Pair Medium (`500`) or Semi-Bold (`600`) headers directly with Regular (`400`) supporting body text.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on an 8-point structural system, maintaining alignment across data panes, inspection panels, and automation workflows.

### Grid Foundations
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column layout with `24px` (`1.5rem`) gutters and variable margins with a `1440px` maximum viewport content boundary.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout with `16px` gutters and `24px` margins. Sidebars collapse to icon rails or off-canvas drawers.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with `16px` gutters and `16px` (`1rem`) edge margins. Full-width horizontal card stacking.

### Spacing Application
- `space-xs` (4px): Micro-spacing within badges, icon-to-label gaps, and pill counters.
- `space-sm` (8px): Input field inner padding, menu item gaps, and inline badge clusters.
- `space-md` (16px): Standard inner card padding, table cell vertical rhythm, and stacked form groups.
- `space-lg` (24px): Card headers, data card separation, and structural panel padding.
- `space-xl` (40px): Section headers, layout division tiers, and top-level canvas orchestration.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is conveyed through flat structural outlines paired with light, multi-stop atmospheric drop shadows. Rather than harsh z-index stacking, depth relies on surface contrast and perimeter strokes.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas):** `#F4F7FB` background, flat surface, no shadow.
- **Level 1 (Card & Panel Surface):** Solid white `#FFFFFF` surface with a continuous 1px stroke of `#E3ECF7`, accompanied by shadow:
  `0px 1px 3px rgba(6, 27, 49, 0.04), 0px 4px 12px rgba(6, 27, 49, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Hovered Cards & Interactive Blocks):** Subtle lift using:
  `0px 4px 6px -1px rgba(6, 27, 49, 0.06), 0px 10px 24px -2px rgba(6, 27, 49, 0.04)`.
  Border darkens slightly to `#CBD5E1`.
- **Level 3 (Flyouts, Popovers, & Menus):** Pure white background with border `#E2E8F0` and diffuse floating shadow:
  `0px 10px 30px -4px rgba(6, 27, 49, 0.08), 0px 4px 8px -2px rgba(6, 27, 49, 0.04)`.
- **Level 4 (Modals & Critical Overlays):** Deep layered elevation paired with a backdrop overlay of `#061B31` tinted at 35% opacity with a `4px` blur:
  `0px 24px 48px -12px rgba(6, 27, 49, 0.18)`.

### Border Integrity
Every elevated card, flyout, and modal maintains an outer 1px hairline border to preserve crisp edges against varying display gamuts.

## Shapes

The geometric architecture relies on a **Soft (Level 1)** hierarchy, keeping elements engineered and crisp rather than playful.

### Corner Radii Guidelines
- **Micro Radii (4px):** Form inputs, standard buttons, badge tags, inline code snippets, and table check cells.
- **Default Structural Radii (8px / `rounded-lg`):** Cards, document viewer panes, intelligence panels, metrics containers, and dropdown menus.
- **Pill (9999px / `rounded-full`):** Reserved exclusively for status indicators, category filters, confidence tags, and user profile badges.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** `#635BFF` fill, solid white text, 1px subtle top highlight (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`). Height: 36px (default), padding: `0 14px`. Radius: 4px. On hover: background transitions to `#533AFD`.
- **Secondary:** `#FFFFFF` fill, `#061B31` text, 1px border `#E3ECF7`. Shadow: Level 1. On hover: background changes to `#FBFCFD` with border color `#CBD5E1`.
- **Ghost/Tertiary:** Transparent fill, `#50617A` text. On hover: `#F4F7FB` fill, `#061B31` text.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- **Status Pills:** Height 22px, radius 9999px, padding: `2px 8px`. Typography: `label-sm`.
  - *Success (Processed):* `#ECFDF5` background, `#059669` text, 1px border `#A7F3D0`.
  - *In-Progress (Extracting):* `#EEF2FF` background, `#4F46E5` text, 1px border `#C7D2FE`.
  - *Alert (Flagged):* `#FEF2F2` background, `#DC2626` text, 1px border `#FECACA`.
- **Interactive Document Filters:** Radius 9999px, `#FFFFFF` background, `#50617A` text, 1px border `#E3ECF7`. Active state switches to `#061B31` background with `#FFFFFF` text.

### Inputs & Form Fields
- Height 36px, background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px `#E3ECF7`, radius 4px. Typography: `body-md` with `#061B31` text, placeholder `#94A3B8`.
- Focus state: `outline: none; border-color: #635BFF; box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(99, 91, 255, 0.15)`.

### Cards & Automation Panes
- Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px perimeter border `#E3ECF7`, 8px corner radius.
- Optional gradient ribbon accent along the top edge: 2px height, spanning 100% width, transitioning from `#635BFF` to `#00D4FF`.
- Section headers within cards utilize 16px vertical padding and are separated by a 1px border `#F1F5F9`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Checkboxes: 16px square, radius 3px, border 1.5px `#CBD5E1`. Checked state: `#635BFF` fill, crisp white check mark icon.
- Focus-visible rings adhere strictly to `rgba(99, 91, 255, 0.3)`.

### Document Data Grids
- Flat header: `#FBFCFD`, 1px border bottom `#E3ECF7`. Header labels use `label-sm` with `#50617A` text uppercase.
- Cell padding: 12px vertical, 16px horizontal. Alternating rows remain plain white; hover row state uses `#F8FAFC`.

### Confidence Score Indicators
- Displayed as a tabular mono label accompanied by a 4px mini progress track: track `#E2E8F0`, indicator bar `#635BFF` (or `#059669` for scores above 95%).
