FAQ

Questions pharma teams ask us

Can Star verify a COA against our own specification?

Yes. You load your specifications once, by material and supplier. The bot reads each COA and checks every result against your limits and units, and flags missing tests or results reported only as “complies”.

Does Star check against USP, Ph. Eur. and JP?

Yes. It records which pharmacopeia and edition the supplier tested to, checks that against what your spec and order require, and flags tests that are missing for the required monograph.

Does this replace our identity testing under 21 CFR 211.84?

No. You still perform at least one identity test on each component. Star checks the COA thoroughly and compares it with the supplier’s history, which supports your program for validating supplier results and decides which lots need more testing.

How does Star spot a supplier changing its specification?

It compares each COA with that supplier’s earlier COAs for the same material. Widened limits, new methods and changed test lists are flagged, and the quality agreement is checked for a required change notice.

Is Star suitable for a GMP environment?

Star is set up for GMP use: a person approves release, every extracted value links to its source, spec checks are rule-based, and every action is logged with user, time and reason. Its controls support Part 11 and Annex 11 compliance, which also depends on how you validate and use it.

Can we validate it?

Yes. Star runs on AWS, Azure or on-premise, so updates follow your change control. We work with your validation team on a risk-based approach, and the same COA always produces the same checks.

Can Star find every batch that used a supplier lot?

Yes. Lots are linked from COA to receipt, batch and product. A where-used search returns every batch, holds the open ones and lists what was released.

Does Star handle supplier qualification documents?

Yes. Questionnaires, quality agreements, BSE/TSE, allergen, residual solvent, elemental impurity and nitrosamine statements are read, checked and tracked for expiry.

Does Star work for dietary supplement makers under 21 CFR 111?

Yes. It checks COAs against your specifications and tracks the supplier qualification evidence you need before relying on a COA, alongside the identity testing the rule requires for dietary ingredients.

Who is in control of the decisions?

You are. You set what can be approved automatically, the tolerances and value limits, and what must always go to a person. Every decision is logged with the reason and the source document.

Will it work with our ERP?

Star posts to SAP, Oracle NetSuite, Oracle Fusion, Microsoft Dynamics 365, Acumatica and Sage, and connects to other systems through REST API, managed file transfer (SFTP) or email. Tell us your system on the form and we’ll show the exact integration in the demo.

Can Star run in our own cloud or on-premise?

Yes. Star can be hosted on AWS or Azure, or installed on-premise so your IT team controls updates through its own change process.

What does it cost?

Pricing is based on pages processed. Plans start at $650 a month for 3,000 pages, and there’s a free tier for 100 pages a month so you can test on your own documents.

How long does it take to go live?

Most teams start with one document type and are live in weeks. New workflows are configured in Star’s Workflow Studio without changing your ERP.
