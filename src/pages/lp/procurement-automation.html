Quality and compliance document verification

Star reads the quality documents that arrive with material: test certificates (MTRs, mill test certificates, EN 10204 3.1 and 3.2 inspection certificates), certificates of analysis (COAs), certificates of conformance and inspection reports. Each one is matched to the purchase order and received lot, and every chemical, mechanical and physical value is checked against the ordered specification (ASTM, ASME, EN, customer spec). Material that fails, or arrives without the right certificate, is held in the ERP before it is used or shipped, and your quality assurance team sees why.

WorkflowTest cert / COA / CoC received→AI extraction→Spec & PO check→Hold or release→ERP lot status + cert filed
  • Verifies heat number, grade, dimensions, chemistry and mechanicals (yield, tensile, elongation, hardness, impact) against the spec limits.
  • Covers test certificates for steel, aluminum and alloys, COAs for chemicals, resins, pharma ingredients and food-grade materials, and certificates of conformance for purchased components.
  • Supports quality assurance: raises a non-conformance on a failed result, records it against the supplier, and feeds incoming-inspection and supplier quality scores.
  • Links each certificate to the PO line and receiving lot so traceability is complete without manual filing.
  • Writes the result back as lot status (released, blocked, hold) in SAP, Dynamics 365, NetSuite, Acumatica, Invera and others.

How it works

How does AI procurement automation work?

Every workflow follows the same five steps, so the second and third workflows you add reuse the rules, approvals and integrations from the first.

Step 1

Input documents

Quotes, POs, certificates, onboarding packs and invoices arrive by email, upload, SFTP, supplier portal or API, in any layout.

Step 2

AI extraction

Agents read each document, identify the type, and extract line items, values and terms with a source location for every field.

Step 3

Validation

Extracted data is checked against your rules, specs and ERP records: PO lines, vendor master, item master, tolerances and spec limits.

Step 4

Exception handling

Anything that fails or falls below confidence goes to the right person with the difference highlighted. Everything else proceeds without a touch.

Step 5

ERP integration

Validated results are written to SAP, NetSuite, Dynamics 365, Acumatica, Coupa, Epicor or Invera as POs, vendor records, lot statuses or posted invoices.

Glossary

Procurement automation terms, explained

RFQ (request for quotation)
A buyer's request asking suppliers to quote price, lead time and terms for specified items.
Mill test report (MTR)
A certificate from the producing mill listing the chemical and mechanical properties of a specific heat of metal.
EN 10204 3.1 certificate
An inspection certificate, validated by the manufacturer's independent inspection department, confirming test results for the delivered product.
Certificate of analysis (COA)
A supplier document reporting test results for a batch or lot of material against its specification.
Certificate of conformance (CoC)
A supplier's statement that delivered goods meet the purchase order and specification requirements.
Order acknowledgement
A supplier's confirmation of a purchase order, including accepted quantities, prices and promise dates.
Three-way match
Checking a supplier invoice against the purchase order and the goods receipt before payment.
Vendor master
The ERP record holding a supplier's legal, tax, banking, payment and contact details.

The basics

What is AI procurement automation?

AI procurement automation uses AI agents to handle the document work in procurement: reading supplier quotes, purchase orders, order confirmations, onboarding forms, test certificates and invoices; checking them against your ERP, specifications and past history; and deciding what happens next. Clean transactions flow straight into the ERP, and only exceptions reach a buyer, quality engineer or AP clerk. It works alongside your ERP or procure-to-pay system rather than replacing it.

How is AI procurement automation different from OCR or RPA?

OCR turns a page into text, and RPA repeats fixed clicks. Neither understands what the document means. Star's AI agents read the document in context, verify every value against the PO, vendor master, specification or contract, compare it with past history such as previous prices and supplier performance, and make or recommend a decision: approve, hold, route or reject. The output is a validated business action, not just extracted text.

Is Star Software a procure-to-pay or ERP system?

No. Star Software is an AI document and workflow automation layer that sits on top of your existing ERP or procurement system. It reads, verifies and processes the documents that flow through procurement and writes the validated results into SAP, NetSuite, Microsoft Dynamics 365, Acumatica, Coupa, Epicor, Invera and other systems through integrations.

Which procurement tasks can be automated with AI?

The tasks with the most document handling benefit most: supplier quote comparison, purchase order creation and order confirmation checks, supplier onboarding, verification of quality documents such as test certificates and COAs, sorting procurement emails and attachments, and three-way invoice matching. Star covers all six on one workspace.

Will AI procurement automation replace buyers?

No. It removes the reading, re-keying and cross-checking that take up much of a buyer's day. People still own supplier relationships, negotiation, sourcing strategy and every exception the agent flags, with the evidence already gathered for them.

Send RFQ responses to a monitored mailbox or upload them. Can AI create purchase orders automatically?

Yes. From an approved requisition or an awarded quote, Star creates the purchase order in your ERP with vendor, item, price and cost-center lookups from your master data. Approval rules still apply, so POs above your limits wait for the right person.

How does AI check supplier order confirmations against purchase orders?

Each order acknowledgement is read and compared with the PO line by line: item, quantity, unit price, delivery date and terms. Matching confirmations are filed automatically, changed promise dates update the PO so planning sees them, and price or quantity differences go to the buyer with the difference highlighted.

How do you automate three-way matching for supplier invoices?
