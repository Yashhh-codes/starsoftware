FAQ

Supplier onboarding automation questions

Answers to the questions procurement and finance teams ask most, including the ones they ask AI assistants.

The basics

What is supplier onboarding?

Supplier onboarding is the process of collecting a new supplier's legal, tax, banking, insurance and quality information, verifying it, getting the right approvals and creating the supplier in your ERP so it can receive purchase orders and payments. Vendor onboarding means the same thing; procurement teams tend to say supplier, finance teams vendor.

What is supplier onboarding automation?

Supplier onboarding automation uses AI agents to collect a new supplier's documents, read them, verify the details against official sources and your own records, route the approval, and create the vendor in your ERP. People only handle the exceptions, such as an expired certificate or a bank account that does not match the supplier's legal name.

Why is supplier onboarding slow?

Documents arrive in pieces and in different formats, someone has to chase what is missing, details are typed into the ERP by hand, and checks such as TIN matching, sanctions screening and bank verification are run separately by different teams. Each hand-off adds days.

What should a supplier onboarding checklist include?

At minimum: a W-9 (or W-8 for foreign suppliers), a bank letter or voided cheque, a certificate of insurance meeting your limits, signed terms or an MSA, and contact details for orders and remittance. Depending on the supplier, add quality certificates (ISO 9001, IATF 16949, AS9100), sample test certificates or COAs, compliance declarations (conflict minerals, RoHS, REACH), safety data sheets and an NDA. Then verify the tax ID, sanctions status, bank ownership and duplicates before approval.

Which onboarding documents can Star read?

Tax forms (W-9, W-8BEN-E), bank letters and voided cheques, certificates of insurance and endorsements, quality certificates (ISO 9001, IATF 16949, AS9100), sample test certificates and COAs, signed supplier terms and NDAs, supplier questionnaires, conflict minerals reports (CMRT), RoHS and REACH declarations, safety data sheets and business registration documents. Documents can arrive as PDFs, scans, photos, spreadsheets or email bodies.

Verification and risk

How do you verify a supplier's W-9 and tax ID?

Star extracts the legal name, tax classification, address and TIN from the W-9 and checks that the name and TIN combination matches IRS records, so 1099 reporting and backup withholding problems are caught before the first payment.

How does Star verify supplier bank details?

Star checks that the account holder on the bank letter matches the supplier's legal name, validates routing, IBAN and SWIFT formats, checks the account is not already used by another vendor or an employee, and can require a callback to a phone number already on file before the details are activated. Bank detail changes follow the same controls, with a second approver.

How do you prevent vendor bank fraud during onboarding and bank changes?

Never accept bank details from email alone. Star checks the account holder against the supplier's legal name, looks for accounts shared with other vendors or employees, detects look-alike sender domains, creates a callback task to a phone number already on file, requires a second approver and can hold the first payment to a new account for review.

Which sanctions and watch lists can be screened?

Screening can be configured against the U.S. OFAC lists and other government lists your compliance team requires, such as EU, UK and UN consolidated lists and denied-party lists. Possible matches are shown to a reviewer with the reason for the match; suppliers are re-screened on a schedule you choose.

What is the difference between supplier onboarding and supplier qualification?

Onboarding makes a supplier able to transact: legal, tax, banking and insurance. Qualification proves it can supply to your quality requirements: certifications such as ISO 9001, IATF 16949 or AS9100, a quality questionnaire, and first-article or PPAP evidence. Quality-critical suppliers need both, and Star runs them in one workflow with QA sign-off.

How are certificate expiry dates tracked?

Every certificate's validity dates are extracted when it arrives. Star sends reminders to the supplier before expiry, reads the renewal when it comes back, and can block new purchase orders in affected categories if a certificate lapses.

Systems and rollout

Can different types of supplier follow different workflows?

Yes. Rules are set per supplier category, country and risk level. A domestic raw-material mill, an overseas component maker and an on-site contractor can each have their own document list, checks, approvers and ERP settings, all on the same workspace.

Can AI onboard international suppliers?

Yes. Star reads W-8BEN-E forms, validates VAT and company registration numbers, checks IBAN and SWIFT formats, screens against the sanctions lists your policy requires, and sets currency, payment method and withholding tax codes on the vendor record.

Which ERP and procurement systems does supplier onboarding integrate with?

SAP S/4HANA and ECC, Oracle NetSuite, Microsoft Dynamics 365, Acumatica, Oracle, Epicor and Invera, procurement platforms such as Coupa, and other systems through REST APIs, flat files and SFTP. Star reads the vendor or supplier master to detect duplicates and writes the approved vendor record back, with every field linked to its source document.

Does Star work with Coupa supplier onboarding?

Yes. Star reads the documents suppliers submit, runs the verification checks and updates the supplier record in Coupa, so your team reviews exceptions instead of re-keying information from attachments.

What is the best supplier onboarding software for manufacturers?

Look for software that reads every document in the pack, not just forms; verifies tax ID, sanctions and bank ownership automatically; handles quality certificates for industrial suppliers; tracks expiry dates; and writes the approved vendor straight to your ERP with an audit trail. Star Software is built for manufacturing, distribution and metals companies with exactly these needs.

How long does it take to go live?

A standard onboarding workflow typically goes live in two to four weeks, depending on ERP access and how many supplier categories you start with. Most teams begin with one category, such as raw-material suppliers, and add others once the rules are proven.
