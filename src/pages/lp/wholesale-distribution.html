FAQ

Questions distribution teams ask us

Can Star read orders from email bodies, PDFs and faxes?

Yes. Orders typed in the email body, attached PDFs, spreadsheets and faxes are all read and entered into your ERP without re-keying.

How does Star match customer part numbers to our SKUs?

It cross-references customer and competitor part numbers and descriptions to your catalog, learns each customer’s numbering over time, and routes anything it can’t match to a rep.

Does Star handle units of measure like case vs each?

Yes. Quantities are converted to how you stock and sell each item, and unusual quantities are flagged against the customer’s order history.

Will it check prices against our contracts?

Yes. Every line is checked against contract, job and price-file pricing. Differences go to the rep before the order is released.

Can Star help with SPA, ship-and-debit and rebate claims?

Yes. Sales are matched to special pricing agreements and rebate programs, and claims are prepared before each vendor’s deadline, so earned money isn’t left behind.

Does Star work with Prophet 21, Eclipse or SX.e?

Star connects to distribution ERPs through REST API, managed file transfer or email, and posts natively to SAP, NetSuite, Oracle, Dynamics 365, Acumatica and Sage. Tell us your ERP on the form and we’ll show the exact integration in the demo.

Can Star handle RFQs and takeoffs?

Yes. Hundreds of lines are matched to your catalog at once, substitutes are suggested for no-match lines, and a draft quote goes to the rep to approve.

How does Star help AR?

It reads remittances, applies payments across invoices, codes short-pays and deductions by reason, and checks credit before releasing held orders.

Who is in control of the decisions?

You are. You set what can be approved automatically, the tolerances and value limits, and what must always go to a person. Every decision is logged with the reason and the source document.

Will it work with our ERP?

Star posts to SAP, Oracle NetSuite, Oracle Fusion, Microsoft Dynamics 365, Acumatica and Sage, and connects to other systems through REST API, managed file transfer (SFTP) or email. Tell us your system on the form and we’ll show the exact integration in the demo.

Can Star run in our own cloud or on-premise?

Yes. Star can be hosted on AWS or Azure, or installed on-premise so your IT team controls updates through its own change process.

What does it cost?

Pricing is based on pages processed. Plans start at $650 a month for 3,000 pages, and there’s a free tier for 100 pages a month so you can test on your own documents.

How long does it take to go live?

Most teams start with one document type and are live in weeks. New workflows are configured in Star’s Workflow Studio without changing your ERP.
