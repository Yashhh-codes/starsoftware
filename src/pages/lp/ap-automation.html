Accounts payable automation, defined

What is an AP agent, and how is it different from AP automation?

AP agent

Reads the context and makes the call.

An AP agent is AI software that runs the accounts payable process end to end. It takes in supplier invoices, extracts the data, verifies the vendor, matches each invoice to the purchase order and goods receipt, checks contract pricing and company policy, decides what to do, explains the decision, and posts the result to the ERP. People handle only the exceptions it routes to them.

  • Decides and explains
  • End to end, into the ERP
  • People see exceptions only
vs
AP automation

Moves invoices through fixed steps.

AP automation (accounts payable automation) is the broader practice of replacing manual accounts payable steps with software: keying invoices, checking for duplicates, matching, GL coding, chasing approvals, scheduling payments and posting. Traditional AP automation software moves invoices through fixed workflow steps and leaves the judgement calls to people; an AP agent reads the surrounding context and makes the call, asking a person only when its confidence is below the threshold you set.

Invoice fraud detection

Invoice fraud detection in AP automation: eight checks before a dollar moves.

Most invoice fraud is quiet: a bank account changed by email, an invoice sent twice, a total nudged up, a vendor that was never real. The AP agent runs these checks on every invoice, and an invoice that fails any of them is held before it reaches the ERP.

  1. Duplicate and double billing

    Exact and fuzzy matching against paid history, across every channel.

  2. Bank-detail change hold

  3. Vendor authentication

    Name, tax ID and bank checked against the master and W-9; sanctions screened.

    PDF metadata, fonts and edit history checked for changes after issue.

    Amount, frequency and unit prices compared with the vendor's own history.

  7. Match and contract evidence

  8. Segregation of duties

    Whoever edits a vendor can't approve or pay it. Every release logged.

Rules

How does the AP agent learn your AP policy?

You don't rebuild your policy as workflow diagrams. The documents your controller already keeps, the AP policy, supplier terms and the approval matrix, are read the same way invoices are, and each clause becomes a rule in the Modify Rules screen with the sentence it came from attached.

Where do the rules come from?
From your own documents. The agent proposes a rule per clause; nothing is active until someone on your team switches it on.
What happens before a rule goes live?
It is run against your last 30 days of invoices so you can see exactly which ones it would have posted, routed or held, before it touches a live one.
Who maintains them?
Finance, in plain language. Change a tolerance or an approver and the next invoice follows the new rule; the old version stays in the audit trail.

Learning

Does the AP agent get more accurate over time?

Yes, and in a way you can see. The agent only asks a person when its confidence is below the threshold you set. Each answer is stored as a named rule with the person who gave it, so the same question is never asked twice and the exception rate keeps falling.

What decides whether a person is asked?
A confidence score on every decision. Above your threshold the invoice posts; below it the agent stops and shows the evidence. It does not guess.
What happens to the answer?
It becomes a rule in the same screen as your policy rules, with the invoice it came from and the colleague who answered. You can edit or retire it like any other.
What does that look like after two months?
In a typical rollout, exceptions fall from around 40 per 100 invoices in week one to single digits by week eight, with the remaining ones concentrated in new vendors and contract changes.

Three generations of AP software

Turns a PDF into fields. Accuracy depends on templates per supplier, and every decision after capture is still a person's job.

AP agent and AP automation FAQ

AP automation and AP agent questions finance teams ask

What is an AP agent?
AI software that runs the accounts payable process end to end: intake, extraction, vendor verification, matching, contract and policy checks, decision, and posting to the ERP. People handle only the exceptions it routes to them.
What is AP automation?
The use of software to replace the manual steps in accounts payable: keying invoices, checking duplicates, matching to POs and receipts, GL coding, chasing approvals, scheduling payments and posting to the ERP.
What is the difference between an AP agent and AP automation software?
AP automation software moves invoices through fixed workflow steps and leaves judgement calls to people. An AP agent reads the surrounding context (PO, receipt, contract, vendor file, policy), makes the decision, explains it, and asks a person only when its confidence is below the threshold you set.
How accurate is Star's AP agent?
Star targets 98% field-level extraction accuracy and reaches 99.3% on invoices in production, without supplier templates. Values below the confidence threshold go to a person, never a guess.
Does it do 2-way and 3-way matching?
Yes. Each invoice is matched two-way to the purchase order and three-way to the goods receipt, line by line, within the price and quantity tolerances you configure.
Which ERPs does it integrate with?
SAP S/4HANA, NetSuite, Microsoft Dynamics 365, QuickBooks, Sage Intacct, Oracle and Invex, plus Salesforce, Snowflake and a REST API. Validated data posts directly and every sync is logged.
How does it prevent duplicate and fraudulent payments?
Every invoice is checked against paid history with exact and fuzzy matching; bank-detail changes are held until verified against the vendor file; nothing posts until the checks pass, and each decision carries an audit entry.
What does AP automation for manufacturing need to handle?
Mostly PO-backed supplier invoices: line-level matching to the purchase order and goods receipt, partial and multi-PO deliveries, coding of materials, tooling, MRO and freight to the right accounts and plant cost centers, and routing only variances to the buyer who owns the PO.
How is AP automation different for the metals and steel industry?
Invoices are priced per ton, CWT or pound, carry surcharges and index-linked contract pricing, and are tied to a heat or lot with a test certificate (MTR or COA). The agent matches invoiced weight to scale tickets and receiving, checks the supply agreement, and holds payment until the certificate is on file.
What does AP automation for wholesale distribution include?
Capture from supplier portals, EDI and email; reconciliation against receiving by branch; credits, rebates and promotional price checks; approval routing by location; and early-payment discount capture at volume.
How long does it take to go live?
Most teams process their first live invoices within two weeks. Suppliers keep sending invoices exactly as they do today.
