FAQ

Questions fabrication teams ask us

Can Star read a 100-page RFQ package, including drawings?

Yes. The bots read the full package at once: drawing sets, spec books, BOMs, addenda, contract terms and support documents. They build a take-off from the drawings and BOM, list every requirement from the spec book, and give your estimator a draft quote with conflicts and risks flagged.

What does Star pull out of the spec book?

Anything that affects cost or risk: welding code, coating system, inspection and testing (UT, MT, RT), certifications such as AISC, tolerances, submittals, Buy America or DFARS requirements, liquidated damages, retainage, payment terms and escalation clauses.

How does Star handle addenda and revised drawings?

Each addendum is compared with the previous version. Changed sheets, quantities, grades and dates are listed, the draft quote is updated, and anything unclear becomes a drafted RFI for the buyer.

Can Star help us decide which jobs to bid?

Yes. The bots score each RFQ on fit with your past wins, your win rate with that customer, current shop load and margin potential. The owner or estimator makes the final call, with the reasons in front of them.

Does Star use our past jobs to price new quotes?

Yes. It compares each RFQ with similar finished jobs, including quoted vs actual hours and material, so new quotes start from real numbers. Your estimator always reviews and approves the price.

How does Star help with change orders, progress billing and retainage?

Scope changes from revised drawings, RFIs and emails are caught and priced with the evidence attached. Pay applications are built from fabricated and shipped work, and retainage is tracked by job so it’s claimed at closeout.

Can Star verify MTRs and stop bad material reaching the shop?

Yes. Every mill cert is checked against ASTM, ASME, API or the job’s own spec. If a value fails, the receipt goes on hold before the steel is cut. If a heat is flagged later, the bot finds every job that used it and holds the open ones.

Can Star prepare shipping documents and data books?

Yes. Packing lists, BOLs and cert packs are created by piece mark, data books are built as work happens, and signed delivery tickets are read back in to trigger the invoice.

Who is in control of the decisions?

You are. Estimators approve every quote, and you set what can be approved automatically and what must go to a person. Every decision is logged with the reason and the source document.

Does it work with JobBOSS, Epicor, Global Shop or our ERP?

Star posts to SAP, Oracle NetSuite, Oracle Fusion, Microsoft Dynamics 365, Acumatica and Sage, and connects to other ERPs through API, SFTP or email. Tell us your ERP on the form and we'll show the exact integration in the demo.

Will this replace our estimators?

No. It removes the copy-and-paste so your estimators can quote more jobs and spend their time on pricing and strategy. That matters even more as experienced estimators retire.

What does it cost?

Pricing is based on pages processed. Plans start at $650 a month for 3,000 pages, and there's a free tier for 100 pages a month so you can test on your own documents.

How long does it take to go live?

Most shops start with one bot, such as quotes or certs, and are live in weeks. There's no change to your ERP and no new system for your team to learn.
