FAQ

Questions service center teams ask us

Does Star only extract data from documents?

No. Extraction is the first of four steps. Star bots then verify each value against your contracts, specs and POs, compare it with that customer’s or mill’s history, and decide whether to approve, route or hold it, with the reason logged.

How does Star use our past history?

The bots learn from your past orders, quotes, invoices, certs and payments. They know each customer’s usual items and prices, each mill’s billing habits and each customer’s payment pattern, and they flag anything that breaks the pattern.

Who is in control of the decisions?

You are. You set what can be approved automatically, the price, margin and value limits, and what must always go to a person. Every decision is logged with the reason and the source document.

Which standards can Star verify mill test reports against?

ASTM, ASME, API and EN 10204, plus your customers’ own specifications. Star reads chemistry, mechanical properties, dimensions and heat treatment from each MTR and checks every value against the minimums and maximums in the spec the order calls for, such as A36, A572-50, A500-C, A106-B or API 5L X52.

Can we add our customers’ own specs, not just ASTM or ASME?

Yes. Customer specs often tighten the standard, for example a lower carbon equivalent, a narrower yield range or a melt-and-pour requirement. You load them once, link them to the customer or part, and the bot checks every cert for that customer against the stricter limits.

What happens when a cert doesn’t match the spec?

The bot puts the receipt on hold in your ERP before the material can be allocated or cut, shows which value failed and by how much, and drafts a request to the mill for a corrected cert. Quality can release or reject the hold with one click, and the decision is logged.

How does Star handle material traceability?

Every heat is linked from the mill cert to the master coil or plate, through each processed tag, to the customer order, packing list and shipment. Anyone can search a heat number and see everywhere it was used, and the right cert goes out with every shipment.

If one heat has a problem, can Star stop it reaching other customers?

Yes. When a heat is flagged, for example from a customer complaint or a failed retest, the bot runs a where-used search, holds every open job and order for other customers that uses that heat, and lists the shipments that have already gone out so your team can contact those customers first.

Can Star read emailed POs where items are written in plain language?

Yes. The order bot reads descriptions like “16ga CRS 48x120” or “3/8 A36 burned to print” and maps them to your part numbers. Lines it isn't sure about are flagged for a rep to confirm before the order is created.

How does Star handle mill test reports from many different mills?

There are no templates to build. The bot reads MTRs from any mill layout, including scans, and extracts heat numbers, chemistry, mechanicals and spec. It checks the values against the spec and links the cert to the PO, inventory and every processed piece cut from that master coil.

Will it work with our metals ERP?

Star posts to SAP, Oracle NetSuite, Oracle Fusion, Microsoft Dynamics 365, Acumatica and Sage, and connects to other ERPs through API, SFTP or email. Tell us your ERP on the form and we'll show the exact integration in the demo.

Does a person still approve quotes and orders?

Yes, as much as you want. Many service centers let clean orders flow straight through and keep quotes rep-approved. Every exception is shown with the source document side by side.

How long does it take to go live?

Most teams start with one document type, such as POs or MTRs, and are live in weeks, not months. There's no change to your ERP and no new system for reps to learn.

What does it cost?

Pricing is based on pages processed. Plans start at $650 a month for 3,000 pages, and there's a free tier for 100 pages a month so you can test on your own documents.

Is our data secure?

Documents are processed in the cloud with encryption in transit and at rest. Your data is never used to train models for other customers. We'll share our security pack on request.
