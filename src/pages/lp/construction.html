FAQ

Questions construction teams ask us

Can Star read a full bid package with plans and specs?

Yes. The bots read plan sets, spec books, addenda, bid forms and contracts together, list your scope by sheet and spec section, and give your estimator a draft bid with gaps, conflicts and risks flagged.

Does it work for subcontractors as well as general contractors?

Yes. Steel erectors, rebar and miscellaneous metals subs, MEP and other specialty contractors use the same bots to find their scope, review subcontracts, and manage pay apps, waivers and compliance documents.

Which contract clauses does Star flag?

Pay-if-paid and pay-when-paid, liquidated damages, retainage terms, indemnity, no-damages-for-delay, insurance requirements and escalation clauses. You set your own limits, and the bot flags anything outside them with the page reference.

How does Star handle addenda?

Each addendum is compared with the previous set. Changed sheets, added scope and new dates are listed, the draft bid is updated, and anything unclear becomes a drafted RFI.

Can Star help us decide which jobs to bid?

Yes. It scores each invitation on fit with your past wins, your hit rate with that GC, how quickly they pay, your crew availability and bonding. The owner or estimator makes the final call.

How does Star help with pay apps, retainage and lien waivers?

It checks G702/G703 pay apps against the contract and schedule of values, makes sure backup and waivers are right before submission, and tracks retainage by job so it’s claimed as soon as you’re eligible.

Can Star match supplier invoices to delivery tickets?

Yes. Invoices are matched to commitments, POs and delivery or batch tickets, coded to the job and cost code, and anything missing or duplicated is held.

Does Star handle mill certs and Buy America for DOT jobs?

Yes. Rebar and steel mill certs are checked against ASTM specs and domestic-origin requirements, tied to the job, and assembled into the package the DOT inspector needs.

Can Star track subcontractor COIs and certified payroll?

Yes. COIs are checked for limits, endorsements and expiry, and payments can be held when coverage lapses. Certified payroll reports are checked for wage rates and classifications before submission.

Who is in control of the decisions?

You are. You set what can be approved automatically, the tolerances and value limits, and what must always go to a person. Every decision is logged with the reason and the source document.

Will it work with our ERP?

Star posts to SAP, Oracle NetSuite, Oracle Fusion, Microsoft Dynamics 365, Acumatica and Sage, and connects to other systems through REST API, managed file transfer (SFTP) or email. Tell us your system on the form and we’ll show the exact integration in the demo.

Can Star run in our own cloud or on-premise?

Yes. Star can be hosted on AWS or Azure, or installed on-premise so your IT team controls updates through its own change process.

What does it cost?

Pricing is based on pages processed. Plans start at $650 a month for 3,000 pages, and there’s a free tier for 100 pages a month so you can test on your own documents.

How long does it take to go live?

Most teams start with one document type and are live in weeks. New workflows are configured in Star’s Workflow Studio without changing your ERP.
