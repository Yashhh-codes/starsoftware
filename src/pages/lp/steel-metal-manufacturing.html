FAQ

Questions metal manufacturing teams ask us

Does Star only extract data from certs and invoices?

No. Extraction is the first of four steps. Star bots then verify each value against your specs, contracts and POs, compare it with that supplier’s, customer’s or heat’s history, and decide whether to approve, route or hold it, with the reason logged.

How does Star use our past history?

The bots learn from your past certs, receipts, orders, invoices and claims. They know each supplier’s usual grades and billing habits, each customer’s order pattern and dispute history, and the results for every heat, and they flag anything that breaks the pattern.

Who is in control of the decisions?

You are. You set what can be approved automatically, the tolerance and value limits, and what must always go to quality or a buyer. Every decision is logged with the reason and the source document.

Which standards can Star verify certificates against?

ASTM, ASME, API and EN 10204, plus your own material specs and your customers’ specs. Star reads chemistry, mechanical properties, dimensions and heat treatment, and checks every value against the limits for grades such as A500-C, A572-50, A106-B, API 5L X52 or 304L.

Can we add our own and our customers’ specs?

Yes. Internal and customer specs often tighten the standard, for example a lower carbon equivalent, residual element limits or a melt-and-pour requirement. You load them once, link them to the supplier, customer or product, and every cert is checked against the stricter limits.

What happens when an incoming cert doesn’t match the spec?

The bot puts the receipt on hold in your ERP before the material can be used, shows which value failed and by how much, and drafts a request to the supplier for a corrected cert. Quality can release or reject the hold with one click, and the decision is logged.

How does Star handle material traceability?

Every heat is linked from the supplier cert and receipt, through melt, lots, bundles or coils, to the customer order, BOL and shipment. Anyone can search a heat number and see everywhere it was used, and the right cert goes out with every shipment.

If one heat has a problem, can Star stop it reaching other customers?

Yes. When a heat is flagged, for example from a customer complaint or a failed retest, the bot runs a where-used search, holds every open order for other customers that uses that heat, and lists the shipments that have already gone out so your team can contact those customers first.

How can a steel mill automate mill test certificates?

Star's cert bot pulls the certificate for each heat or bundle on an order, checks chemistry and mechanicals against the customer's spec, attaches it to the shipping documents and emails it to the customer. It also answers cert-copy requests automatically and keeps a record of every cert sent.

How do we prove melt-and-pour origin for EU imports and US customs?

From 1 October 2026 the EU requires a mill certificate showing the melt-and-pour country and heat number, and US customs already collects mill certificates for steel. Star pulls melt and pour data from every cert, ties it to the heat number, and flags gaps before the shipment leaves.

Can Star check incoming raw material and alloy certificates?

Yes. The QA bot reads supplier certs in any layout, checks each value against your material specs, links the cert to the receipt and lot, and holds non-conforming material for quality review.

Does it work with SAP, Epicor or PSImetals?

Star posts to SAP, Oracle NetSuite, Oracle Fusion, Microsoft Dynamics 365, Acumatica and Sage, and connects to other ERPs and MES systems through API, SFTP or email. Tell us your system on the form and we'll show the exact integration in the demo.

Is AI accurate enough for quality documents?

Every value is checked against your spec and the source cert, and anything outside limits or unclear is routed to a person with the field highlighted. Your metallurgists keep release authority. Star removes the typing and searching.
